# Bloom & Flourish

Act as an award-winning Senior UI/UX Designer, Creative Director, and Principal Frontend Engineer.

Your task is to design and develop a world-class NGO website that looks worthy of winning Awwwards, CSS Design Awards, and FWA.

The organization focuses on women empowerment, justice reform, prison outreach, education, leadership, and humanitarian work across Kenya and internationally.

The website must feel premium, trustworthy, emotional, elegant, modern, and globally recognized.

Do NOT create a generic NGO website.

Think of organizations like UN Women, World Vision, Charity: Water, UNICEF, Gates Foundation, and Apple-level attention to detail.

The website should immediately inspire trust and encourage visitors to donate, volunteer, partner, or learn more.

Simply Feminine Network is a non-governmental organization in Kenya dedicated to empowering women, improving mental health, and restoring dignity to women and children affected by the criminal justice system.Overview and LeadershipFounded: Established in 2021 by Agnes Vorriter, a Kenyan model and entrepreneur based in Germany.Leadership: Headed by CEO Tabitha Mwelu John.Recognition: Named NGO of the Year at the Pride of Kenya Awards 2025.Core MissionPrison Support: Focuses on helping women and children impacted by the justice system by providing basic essentials like sanitary pads and undergarments to aid in reintegration.Wellbeing: Works to boost self-esteem, positive body image, and mental health awareness among women and girls.Community Outreach: Engages in charitable activities, such as hospital visits and community donation drives. Community ProgramsIMARA HER Project: Officially launched at their second anniversary gala, this program features Kenya’s first solar-powered mobile empowerment lab. The specialized van travels directly to underserved communities in Nairobi, Kitui, and Kisumu to deliver tailored mentorship, vocational training, dignity kits, and economic opportunities to women and girls.International Summits: To bridge the gap between global networks and local action, the network hosts events like the Women’s Leadership and Empowerment Summit at the Schloss Hotel Berlin. Coordinated by founder Agnes Vorreiter, these sessions convene African diaspora leaders, diplomats, and international organizations to invest back into grassroots Kenyan enterprise financing and development.Recent & Upcoming EventsNairobi Grand Ball: This high-profile annual black-tie philanthropic gala serves as a major fundraising vehicle. It brings together vetted corporate brands, diplomats, development partners, and public figures to center conversations on social accountability. The event focuses heavily on championing women’s health, raising endometriosis awareness, and financing programs to combat gender-based violence.Annual Charity Gala (November 28, 2026): The organization will hold its 3rd Annual Simply Feminine Network Charity Gala. Building on previous high-profile galas attended by public leaders like Nominated Senator Gloria Orwoba, this gathering marks the network's next milestone for celebrating community impact and raising program funding.How to Get InvolvedIf your business, development institution, or corporate social responsibility (CSR) department wants to coordinate with their initiatives, you can reach out to the Simply Feminine Network management through their official channels:Email: simplyfemininenetwork@gmail.comKenya Operations Helpline: +254 769 054 165Germany Diaspora Liaison: +49 1511 565 3888
